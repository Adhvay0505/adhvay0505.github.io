I work close to the system.

Linux-first, security-minded, and deeply interested in how things actually behave under the hood.

Most of my time is spent in GNU/Linux, Neovim, and the shell — building, breaking, automating, and analyzing systems with a strong focus on security and reliability. I’m particularly drawn to open-source tooling, detection engineering, and understanding attacks by studying their mechanics rather than just their symptoms.

I sharpen my skills breaking CTFs on TryHackMe, Hack The Box, and pwn boxes.
I prefer infrastructure that disappears once it’s working. A Raspberry Pi running k0s hosts a few services I care about.

I care about clean systems, reproducible setups, thoughtful tooling, and security that respects how real systems are used. Curious by default, opinionated when it matters, and almost always one terminal away from experimenting with something new.
